Who We Are

We are the backbone of visual storytelling in the Telugu Film Industry. Our masters and technicians bring imagination to life through pyrotechnics, mechanical effects, atmospherics, and modernized special effects.

Established to unite and empower the skilled artisans behind the screen, TCTSEMTU stands for safety, innovation, and excellence in cinematic special effects.
